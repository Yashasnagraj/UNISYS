INTRODUCTION
Huntington's disease (HD) is a hereditary neurodegenerative disorder that profoundly affects the cognitive and motor functions of patients, leading to significant challenges in daily living. With an estimated 30,000 individuals affected in the United States alone, there is a pressing need for innovative solutions to facilitate early diagnosis and management of this condition.
Our project, HuntAI, aims to create a comprehensive interface that empowers both patients and healthcare providers by enabling the secure collection and analysis of genetic data related to Huntington's disease. Patients will input their genetic information, which will be processed using advanced machine learning algorithms developed in TensorFlow to identify potential risks associated with HD.
To ensure data integrity and security, we will leverage blockchain technology, allowing for safe storage and transfer of sensitive genetic information. Our system will not only provide real-time insights into genetic predispositions but also facilitate ongoing contributions to the research community, enhancing the understanding of Huntington's disease.
By addressing the critical gaps in current monitoring methods—such as their lack of real-time capabilities—our solution seeks to transform patient care through proactive alerts and personalized treatment recommendations. Ultimately, HuntAI aims to improve patient outcomes by fostering a collaborative environment between patients, caregivers, and healthcare professionals while prioritizing data security and privacy. 










PROBLEM STATEMENT
Huntington's disease (HD) presents significant challenges not only for those diagnosed but also for their families and healthcare providers. The genetic nature of the disease, caused by mutations in the HTT gene, leads to a 50% inheritance risk for offspring of affected individuals. Despite the availability of genetic testing, many patients face difficulties in understanding the implications of their results. Misinterpretation of genetic data can lead to emotional distress and confusion regarding disease management, especially when considering the variable penetrance of the CAG repeat expansions associated with HD. This unpredictability complicates counselling efforts, as healthcare professionals must navigate complex emotional and psychological landscapes while providing accurate information about potential outcomes and risks.
In addition to the emotional and psychological hurdles, there is a pressing need for secure and efficient methods to analyse genetic data. Current systems often lack the integration of advanced technologies that can enhance data integrity and accessibility. By leveraging blockchain technology alongside machine learning algorithms, Hunt AI aims to provide a robust platform for genetic analysis that ensures patient data security while delivering timely insights into disease risk. This project addresses not only the technical aspects of genetic testing but also emphasizes the importance of compassionate communication and informed decision-making in the context of Huntington's disease, ultimately striving to improve patient outcomes and support families facing this challenging diagnosis.

PROPOSED SOLUTION
 
 

The HuntAI project aims to develop an innovative platform that integrates advanced technologies to enhance the analysis and management of genetic data related to Huntington's disease. By creating a user-friendly interface, patients will be empowered to easily upload their genetic information and receive personalized insights regarding their risk of developing HD. This approach not only simplifies the process of genetic testing but also fosters a more informed patient experience, allowing individuals to take an active role in their healthcare decisions.
At the core of our solution is the implementation of machine learning algorithms using TensorFlow and PyTorch. These algorithms will be trained on extensive datasets to accurately analyse genetic markers associated with Huntington's disease. By leveraging techniques such as SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations), we will ensure that the predictions made by our models are interpretable and transparent, helping patients and healthcare providers understand the basis for risk assessments.
To address data security and integrity, HuntAI will utilize blockchain technology alongside IPFS (InterPlanetary File System) for decentralized storage. Blockchain will provide a secure framework for managing patient consent and access to genetic data, ensuring that sensitive information remains confidential and tamper-proof. Meanwhile, IPFS will facilitate efficient storage and retrieval of large genomic datasets, enhancing accessibility while reducing costs associated with traditional cloud storage solutions.In addition to robust data analysis capabilities, HuntAI will prioritize patient education and support by incorporating comprehensive resources within the platform. These resources will include informative content about Huntington's disease, genetic testing implications, and guidance on interpreting results. By fostering an environment of education and support, we aim to empower patients and their families to make informed decisions regarding their health and treatment options.
Ultimately, the HuntAI project seeks to transform the landscape of genetic analysis for Huntington's disease by combining cutting-edge technology with a patient-centered approach. By integrating machine learning, blockchain, and decentralized storage solutions, we aim to enhance the accuracy of risk assessments while ensuring data security and integrity. Through this innovative platform, we hope to improve patient outcomes, facilitate collaboration among healthcare providers, and contribute valuable insights to ongoing research efforts in the field of genetics. patient care by predicting episodes and improving quality of life. 	Data Collection and Processing Workflow:
1. Data Acquisition
The HuntAI project will continuously collect genetic and health-related information from patients, focusing on biomarkers associated with Huntington's disease. This data acquisition will involve various methods, including patient-reported outcomes, genetic testing results, and clinical assessments, ensuring a comprehensive dataset for analysis.

2. Data Transmission
Once collected, the data will be securely transmitted to Firebase using blockchain technology. This approach guarantees high-speed, encrypted data transfer, protecting patient information during transmission and ensuring that it is tamper-proof. The use of blockchain enhances the trustworthiness of the data being processed.
3. Secure Data Storage
The transmitted data will be stored in HIPAA-compliant cloud storage solutions, such as Firebase Firestore or AWS S3. To ensure data privacy and integrity, protocols like TLS/SSL encryption will be employed, safeguarding sensitive genetic information against unauthorized access and breaches.
4. Model Training and Prediction
A logistic regression model will be implemented within Firebase to continuously learn from incoming data. This model will adapt to new patterns in the genetic information, improving its predictive capabilities over time. Additionally, tools like SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME (Local Interpretable Model-agnostic Explanations) will provide insights into model predictions, helping healthcare providers understand the rationale behind risk assessments.
5. Real-Time Alerts and Feedback Loop
The trained model will generate real-time predictions and issue alerts for potential health episodes related to Huntington's disease, facilitating timely intervention by healthcare providers. A continuous feedback loop will allow for incremental updates to the model based on new patient data, maintaining its accuracy and relevance over time while ensuring that it evolves alongside advancements in genetic research.
